# Distribute Research Identity Across Application Surfaces

Vegapunk will remove standalone decorative point-cloud fields and distribute the Research Identity Layer through the Ambient Particle Substrate, structural whitespace, shared local texture, and permitted State Particle Fields.
This replaces the visually dominant Canvas panel because a research workspace gains more from persistent orientation and interaction feedback than from a large non-data-bearing illustration.

## Consequences

- The Discovery Module gives its layout area to actual research context, progress, and artifacts.
- A particle visual is never allocated a dedicated display region unless it is an explicitly labelled visualization backed by real data.
- Stable Particle Identities remain deterministic but are applied to the whole module, high-level groups, and the current object rather than rendered as a single image.
